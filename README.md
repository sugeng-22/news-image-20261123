# The World in One Day — Monday 23 November 2026

A single-image news infographic for the make.fun **News Image** bounty
(`news-image-20261123`). `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; `generate.py` is the Pillow script that rendered the
image from scratch (deterministic, no network).

## What's in the image

- **Diplomacy — Abu Dhabi:** US-mediated Ukraine–Russia talks end without a
  breakthrough; a large prisoner swap is agreed in principle.
- **War at sea:** Ukraine says its sea drones sank the Russian corvette
  Merkury near Benghazi, Libya, and hit a Russian cargo ship in the Baltic.
- **Climate — COP31, Antalya:** the longest UN climate summit ends without a
  pact after collapsing over fossil-fuel wording; UN Secretary-General
  António Guterres says he is "deeply disappointed."
- **US politics:** Democrats lock a House majority of 258 seats, their
  largest since 1992.
- **Tech:** Nvidia shares jump about 30% as founder Jensen Huang returns
  as CEO.
- **Health — DR Congo:** fighting near a hospital in Ituri traps an Ebola
  response team; the outbreak passes 11,700 cases.
- **Also today:** FIFA names England host of the 2035 Women's World Cup;
  the FBI investigates a hack of a US national criminal database; Mexico
  arrests FBI top-10 fugitive "El Chapo Isidro."
